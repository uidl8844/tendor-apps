Tendor Caffeine Log Pro

One click for coffee, espresso, tea, cola, energy drink or mate — or enter your own values. The app adds up the day, estimates the remaining effect with a 5-hour half-life and calculates the latest time for an 80 mg drink before your bedtime.

  • One-click drink presets
  • Daily total against your limit
  • Estimated active caffeine
  • Latest drink time for your bedtime
  • 7-day overview
  • Text and JSON export
